## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/3-70-ratchet/ | kind: description -->

First released in the UX-02 Hells Hammer 3-70H Starter as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Unique Line system, 3-70 (or Three Seventy) is a Ratchet from the Beyblade X generation. The number “ 3 ” refers to the number of blades along the Ratchet’s perimeter, therefore 3-70 has three protrusions/blades. In Beyblade X, Ratchets blades are acting as weak points for burst attacks, therefore players have to make sure the blades of the ratchet are not too exposed. When assembling this Ratchet, it is worth noting that the three blades on 3-80 perimeter line up differently depending on how the Ratchet is positioned. Furthermore, 3-70 protrusions can be aligned with many three-sided Blades contact points, such as Dran Sword , which may improve the combination attack potential. Therefore, it is recommended to always check the alignement of the blades and the contact points when assembling the combination.
