## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/0-60-ratchet/ | kind: description -->

First released with CX-10 Booster Wolf Hunt F0-60DB as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Custom Line on November 1st, 2025, 0-60 (or Zero Sixty) is a Ratchet from the Beyblade X generation. In theory, 0-60 doesn’t have any protrusion, featuring a smooth circular shape. In reality however, three thin blades are protruding vertically, exposing the Ratchet to strikes coming from below. To compensate for its smooth perimeter, 0 Ratchets are wider, making 0 the largest Ratchet available. As a result, in spite of the absence of protrusions, 0 Ratchets can create a lot of “Bit Releases”. A “Bit Release” happens when the Bit is separated from the combo, while the Ratchet is still locked in place. 0-60 is quite heavy, only outweighed by 7-60 . This weight can be felt during Battle, 0-60 tends to add some stability and stamina to the combination. Unfortunately, its tendency to create Bit Releases make it difficult to use in Stamina type combinations. Additionally, 0 having a smooth perimeter, it has decent LAD ( Life-After-Death ). However, the three protruding do have an impact on LAD, preventing 0-60 to completely outclass other options such as 5-60 , 9-60 or 6-60 .
