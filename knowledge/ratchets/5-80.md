## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/5-80-ratchet/ | kind: description -->

First released with the BX-16 Random Booster Viper Tail Select as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports system, 5-80 (or five eighty) is a Ratchet from the Beyblade X generation. The number “ 5 ” refers to the number of blades along the Ratchet’s perimeter, so 5-80 has five blades. In Beyblade X, Ratchets blades are acting as weak points for burst attacks, therefore players have to make sure the blades of the ratchet are not too exposed. Additionally, while 5-80 features more blades than 4-80 , their contact surface is also wider : 8 mm for 5-80 blades against 4 mm for 4-80 blades. So far, nothing indicates that the increased width and number of blades along 5-80 perimeter are increasing stamina potential. In fact, this Ratchet tends to have a negative impact on stamina potential when used with stamina Bits such as Ball .

The following is a provisional note, further testing and more players feedback are required to confirm this hypothesis : Depending on the Blade used, the Ratchet 5-80 can decrease stamina potential. Nonetheless, it tends to improve performances of stationary combinations using pointy Bits such as Needle or High Needle or Spike preventing the Beyblade to wobble and tilt over too early in battle. Therefore, the use of 5-80 in such combo increases stability and stamina potential. However, the five wide blades along 5-80 perimeter can create an increased risk of bursting. Furthermore, 5-80 can improve a combination stamina potential when an unbalanced Blade is used. Both 5-60 and 5-80 Ratchets are used to add balance and stability to a combination. Update (January 2024) : Reports from tournaments are confirming this theory. 5-80 is becoming a popular choice in stationary combinations. It is used to increase the combination’s balance.
