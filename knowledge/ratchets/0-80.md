## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/0-80-ratchet/ | kind: description -->

First released with Ghost Circle 0-80GB from the UX-12 Random Booster Vol. 5 as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Unique Line system on December 28th, 2024, 0-80 (or Zero Eighty) is a Ratchet from the Beyblade X generation. In theory, 0-80 doesn’t have any protrusion, featuring a smooth circular shape. In reality however, three thin blades are protruding vertically, exposing the Ratchet to strikes coming from below. To compensate for its perfectly smooth perimeter, 0 Ratchets are wider, making 0 the largest Ratchet available. As a result, in spite of the absence of protrusions, 0 Ratchets can create a lot of “Bit Releases”. A “Bit Release” happens when the Bit is separated from the combo, while the Ratchet is still locked in place. When a Bit Release occurs, it is registered as a Burst Finish. 0-70 and 0-80 are quite heavy, only outweighed by 7-70 and 7-80 . This weight can be felt during Battle : 0-80 tends to add some stability and stamina to the combination. Unfortunately, its height and the resulting Burst risk make it difficult to use, even in a non competitive environment. Additionally, 0 having a smooth perimeter, in theory it may have decent LAD ( Life-After-Death ). However, the three blades protruding below 0-80 decrease LAD potential, preventing 0 type Ratchets to outclass other options such as 5-60 , 9-60 or 6-60 .
