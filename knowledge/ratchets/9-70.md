## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/9-70-ratchet/ | kind: description -->

First released with Phoenix Rudder 9-70G in the UX-07 Phoenix Rudder Deck Set as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports system on August 10th, 2024, 9-70 (or Nine Seventy) is a Ratchet from the Beyblade X generation.

The number “ 9 ” refers to the number of blades along the Ratchet’s perimeter, which means that 9-70 has 9 blades. In Beyblade X, Ratchets blades are acting as weak points for burst attacks, therefore players have to make sure the blades of the ratchet are not too exposed. On 9-70, out of the 9 blades, 6 are barely protruding from the Ratchet, increasing burst-resistance. Furthermore, the three larger blades on 9-70 can be aligned with Phoenix Wing contact points, which can increase attack potential. More specifically, all three Launcher Hooks are protruding on the Blade perimeter, creating an additional contact point on each wing. Nine Seventy blades can be lined up with these three additional contact points. It is worth noting that the three protrusions on 9-70 perimeter will line up differently depending on how the Ratchet is positioned. Therefore, it is recommended to check the alignement of Nine Seventy protrusions and the Blade contact points when assembling the combination. With only three protrusions featuring a limited contact surface, burst-resistance is further increased.
