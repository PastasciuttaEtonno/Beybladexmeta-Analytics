## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/4-60-ratchet/ | kind: description -->

First released in the BX-02 Hells Scythe 4-60T Starter as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports system on July 15th, 2023, 4-60 (or Four Sixty) is a Ratchet from the Beyblade X generation. The number “ 4 ” refers to the number of protrusions along the Ratchet’s perimeter, so 4-60 has four protrusions. In Beyblade X, Ratchets protrusions are acting as weak points for burst attacks, therefore players have to make sure these protrusions are not too exposed. Additionally, while 4-60 features more protrusions than 3-60 , their contact surface is smaller : 5mm for 4-60 blades against 7mm for 3-60 blades. The number “ 60 ” refers to the Ratchet height, which in this case is 6 mm. This height is considered as “low”, meaning that this Ratchet can be used to more easily strike opponents from below. Furthermore, the “60” height Ratchets have weaker locks than the “80” height Ratchets. However, the lower Ratchet being harder to strike, the lower height slightly mitigates this issue. However, the shape of 4-60 protrusions are easily struck by opposing Beyblades, making 4-60 combinations easy to Burst. At the time of writing, the shape of 4-60 and 2-60 have the worst Burst Resistance in Beyblade X. 4-60 used to be a popular choice in the early days of Beyblade X because of a lack of alternatives. However, this Ratchet disappeared from competitive players Decks as soon as other options became available. Its poor Burst resistance now make it an outclassed option.
