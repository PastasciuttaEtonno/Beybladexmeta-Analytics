## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/0-70-ratchet/ | kind: description -->

First released with the UX-14 Scorpio Spear 0-70 Zap Starter as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Unique Line system on April 26th, 2025, 0-70 (or Zero Seventy) is a Ratchet from the Beyblade X generation. In theory, 0-70 doesn’t have any protrusion, featuring a smooth circular shape. In reality however, three thin blades are protruding vertically, exposing the Ratchet to strikes coming from below. To compensate for its smooth perimeter, 0 Ratchets are wider, making 0 the largest Ratchet available. As a result, in spite of the absence of protrusions, 0 Ratchets can create a lot of “Bit Releases”. A “Bit Release” happens when the Bit is separated from the combo, while the Ratchet is still locked in place. 0-70 and 0-80 are quite heavy, only outweighed by 7-70 and 7-80 . This weight can be felt during Battle, 0-70 tends to add some stability and stamina to the combination. Unfortunately, its height and the resulting Burst risk can make it difficult to use. Additionally, 0 having a smooth perimeter, it has good LAD ( Life-After-Death ). However, the three protruding blades decrease LAD potential, preventing 0-70 to completely outclass other options such as 5-60 , 9-60 or 6-60 .
