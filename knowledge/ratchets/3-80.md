## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/3-80-ratchet/ | kind: description -->

First released in the BX-04 Knight Shield 3-80N Starter as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports system, 3-80 (or Three Eighty) is a Ratchet from the Beyblade X generation. The number “ 3 ” refers to the number of blades along the Ratchet’s perimeter, therefore 3-80 has three blades. In Beyblade X, Ratchets blades are acting as weak points for burst attacks, therefore players have to make sure the blades of the ratchet are not too exposed. When assembling this Ratchet to three-sided Blades such as Dran Sword , it is worth noting that the three blades on 3-80 perimeter line up differently depending on how the Ratchet is positioned. Furthermore, 3-80 protrusions can be aligned with Dran Sword’s contact points, which may increase the combination attack potential. Therefore, it is recommended to always check the alignement of the blades and the contact points when assembling the combination. Additionally, while 3-80 features less blades than 4-80 , their contact surface is wider : 5mm for 4-80 blades against 7mm for 3-80 blades.
