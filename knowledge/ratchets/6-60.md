## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/6-60-ratchet/ | kind: description -->

First released in the CX-01 Dran Brave S6-60V Starter as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Custom Line system on March 29th, 2025, 6-60 (or Six Sixty) is a Ratchet from the Beyblade X generation.

The number “ 6 ” refers to the number of blades along the Ratchet’s perimeter, which means that 6-60 has 6 blades. In Beyblade X, Ratchets protrusions/blades are acting as weak points for burst attacks, therefore players have to make sure the blades of the ratchet are not too exposed. On 6-60, out of the 6 blades, 3 are slightly larger, while the 3 remaining protusions are thinner and smaller. The 6 protrusions create an overall circular design, which may result in increased LAD (Life-After-Death). At the time of writing, 5-60 is the preferred option to create high L.A.D. potential, which is the Bey ability to keep spinning on the stadium floor.
