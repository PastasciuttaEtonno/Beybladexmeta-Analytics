## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/gear-needle-bit/ | kind: description -->

First released in the BX-27 Random Booster Sphinx Cowl Select as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Basic Line system on February 22nd, 2024, Gear Needle (or GN ) is a defense type Bit. It is the “Gear” variant of Needle . A “Gear” variant means that the Bit features extended Gears, making it easier for the combination to ride the X-Line . Gear Needle is a pointy Bit, featuring a conical shape and a sharp tip, albeit not as sharp as its predecessors Needle and High Needle . The shape of the tip results in a docile, controllable stationary behavior. When launched hard, a GN combination will move in small circles inside the center area of the stadium before steadying itself. It is also worth noting that GN has a lower height than Needle and High Needle, making the combination 0,5mm to 1,5mm lower. In terms of defense potential, the low friction of the tip creates poor knock-out resistance. Even though GN tip is not as sharp as its predecessors, a GN combination is still easy to destabilize and knock-out. Additionally, the stationary behavior of this Bit can be very safe, remaining steady in the center of the stadium, thus evading attacks from the opponent. On the other hand, the stationary behavior can also make the combination an easy target for Xtreme Dash attacks. It i also worth noting that when repelled against the X-Line, a GN combination will ride it easily due to the extended gears, creating an Xtreme-Dash and losing stamina in the process. Furthermore, the extended gears also result in an increased self Knock-Out risk. Additionally, the extended Gears create a circular “guard” above the tip, which creates a small amount of LAD . “GN” can be used with any type of Blades, including attack oriented Blades such as Dran Sword , Shark Edge , or Dran Dagger etc.

## Valutazione esterna (opinione)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/gear-needle-bit/ | kind: opinion -->

Intended to be a defense type Bit, Gear Needle features decent pure stamina potential. However, once in Battle, a GN combination will be easily destabilized and knocked-out, with the extended gears increasing self Knock-out risk. Its lower height can make it interesting for Defense type players, although Gear Needle is heavily outclassed for competitive use. Therefore, Gear Needle cannot be recommended to competitive players.
