## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/wedge-bit/ | kind: description -->

First released in the CX-03 Perseus Dark B6-80W Booster as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Custom Line system on March 29th, 2025, Wedge (or W ) is a Defense type Bit. Wedge is a very unique Defense type Bit, featuring a low angle conical tip. The design of the tip results in high stamina potential, and its low angle conical shape also provides decent stability. When launched hard, “W” has a semi-mobile behavior, and may even create sudden accelerations when tilting. This Bit will adopt a stationary behavior in the second part of the Battle. Furthermore, similarly to Rush , Wedge has a reduced number of gears, featuring ten gears instead of the usual twelve. This has two consequences : Xtreme Dash attacks are slower, and the resulting stamina loss is mitigated. Therefore, out of all the available Defense type Bits, Wedge the highest stamina potential. It can easily outperform Bits such as Needle and High Needle , making it one the best Defense type Bits available. However, Wedge pointy tip will also result in decreased knock-out resistance, and it will be easier to destabilize than most of Stamina type Bits, such as Ball . Moreover, combinations using “W” will tilt over earlier than combos using “B”.

## Valutazione esterna (opinione)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/wedge-bit/ | kind: opinion -->

Thanks to its reduced number of gears, similarly to Rush , as well as its tip design, Wedge is one of the best Defense type Bits at the time of writing. Its stability and mitigated stamina losses are providing high stamina potential, making it a quite effective Defense type option. Therefore, while Wedge may not have enough knock-out resistance to be compared with Bits such as Ball and Orb , it is a great addition for players looking for decent defense type options in their Beyblade X collection.
