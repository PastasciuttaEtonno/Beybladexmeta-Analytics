## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/cyclone-bit/ | kind: description -->

First released with the Cobalt Dragoon 2-60C Starter as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Basic Line system on July 13th, 2024, Cyclone (or C ) is an attack type Bit. “C” features a flat, spiral shaped tip going in a counter-clockwise motion. This shape is reminiscent of Left Flat (LF), Meteo L-Drago Performance Tip in Metal Fight Beyblade generation, as well as Spiral, another Driver from the Beyblade Burst generation. This design is intended to create aggressive movements, helping the combination to reach the X-Line and perform the Xtreme Dash . However, the high friction results in poor stamina potential. Additionally, the shaft of this Bit is wider, resulting in increased burst resistance. Overall, the star-shaped tip of Cyclone may feel easier to control than Bits such as Flat , or Low Flat but its results and performances are similar.

## Valutazione esterna (opinione)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/cyclone-bit/ | kind: opinion -->

Featuring poor stamina and offering little control, the sole purpose of this Bit is to burst or knock the opponent out as fast as possible. Being fairly easy to control despite its agressive behavior, C is an enjoyable attack type Bit, although it remains outclassed by options such as Rush and Low Rush for competitive play.
