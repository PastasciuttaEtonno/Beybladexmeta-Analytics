## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/elevate-bit/ | kind: description -->

First released with the BX-36 Random Booster Whale Wave Select as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Basic Line system on September 14th, 2024, Elevate (or E ) is a Balance type Bit. Elevate has a flat tip, with a thin protrusion in its center, surrounded by a thick disc intended to “elevate” the combination during Xtreme Dash attacks. Unlike other Balance type Bits, Elevate doesn’t feature a widened shaft, resulting in poor Burst resistance. The flat tip of “E” is able to create fairly aggressive movements in the early stage of the Battle, while the thin protrusion will help maintaining decent stamina until the end of the Battle. Furthermore, the wide, flat tip provides high stability, which helps any combination to maintain its posture until the very end of the Battle. E’s stability is now only matched by Wall Ball . Moreover, when the Beyblade tilts and is about to fall on the stadium floor, the combination will actually keep spinning on Elevate’s Disc, providing high L.A.D. potential to the combination. The high stability and L.A.D. of Elevate made it the obvious choice for competitive Cobalt Dragoon combinations, allowing Cobalt Dragoon to counter the best stamina and attack type combinations, using Blades such as Silver Wolf , Wizard Arrow , and Aero Pegasus .
