## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/gear-rush-bit/ | kind: description -->

First released with Fox Brush J9-70GR in the CX-06 Random Booster FoxBrush Select as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Custom Line on May 17th, 2025, Gear Rush (or GR ) is an attack type Bit. It is the Gear variant of Rush . A “Gear” variant means that the Bit feature extended Gears, completely covering the tip itself, making it easier for the combination to ride the X-Line and perform Xtreme Dash attacks. “GR” features a round, flat tip. The tip is not as wide as Flat and Low Flat , creating less friction with the stadium floor. Due to the perfectly flat shape of the tip, it has enough mobility and aggressiveness to ride the X-Line and perform Xtreme Dash attacks with ease. Like other attack type Bits, the shaft is wider, resulting in increased burst resistance.

## Valutazione esterna (opinione)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/gear-rush-bit/ | kind: opinion -->

Gear Rush may not be as effective as Rush and Low Rush for competitive use, mainly because of its decreased stamina, and higher self KO risk. However, the reduced number of gears still make GR a relatively safe Bit when compared to other Gear variants such as Gear Flat . Additionally, the increased velocity of Xtreme Dash attacks may have a decent synergy with Blades lacking weight and inertia. Therefore, Gear Rush is a nice addition in any Beyblade X collection.
