## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/low-rush-bit/ | kind: description -->

First released with the UX-11 Impact Drake 9-60LR Starter as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Unique Line system on December 28th, 2024, Low Rush (or LR ) is an Attack type Bit. LR is the low variant of Rush , the most popular option in competitive attack type combinations. Therefore, similarly to Rush, “LR” features a round, flat tip. The tip itself is not as wide as Flat and Low Flat , creating less friction with the stadium floor. However, the flat shape of the tip provides enough mobility and aggressiveness to ride the X-Line and perform the Xtreme Dash with ease. Like other attack type Bits, the shaft is wider, resulting in increased burst resistance.

## Valutazione esterna (opinione)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/low-rush-bit/ | kind: opinion -->

Low Rush is very similar to Rush , the most popular Bit in attack type combinations. This Low variant is just as safe as its standard counterpart. Compared to Rush, LR has better Burst resistance as well as better Burst attack potential, but its lower height can make a LR combination easier to destabilize in very specific match-ups. Therefore, Low Rush is at least a must have for players who enjoy attack type combos. It may also be a very interesting option for competitive players thanks to its ease of use.
