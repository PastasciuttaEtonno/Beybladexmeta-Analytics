## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/orb-bit/ | kind: description -->

First released in the BX-16-01 Random Booster Viper Tail Select as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports system on October 7th, 2023, Orb (or O ) is a stamina type Bit. “O” features a small ball-shaped plastic tip, similar to Ball (B). Compared to Ball, “O” has a smaller diameter, resulting in a smaller contact surface between the Bit and the stadium floor. The smaller contact surface creates less mobility, making combinations using “O” stationary in the center area of the stadium, similarly to Needle or High Needle . When hard launched, a combination using “O” will typically move in small circles before remaining stationary. This docile behavior allows players to launch the combination with force and confidence, and keeps the combination predictable, making “O” a relatively safe option in that regard. However, the lack of mobility can also make the combination easier to strike early in the battle. Furthermore, the smaller contact surface between the Bit and the stadium floor creates less friction, increasing stamina potential. In theory, this means that “O” could have more stamina potential than Ball, and its ball-shaped tip could make it more stable than Needle. However, in reality, “O” stamina potential is outclassed by Ball . When compared to Ball, the smaller diameter of Orb also results in a decreased knock-out resistance. Moreover, it makes it more difficult for the combination to re-stabilize itself, and combinations using “O” will tilt over earlier than combos using “B”. However, the round shape of Orb’s tip makes it far more difficult to destabilize than Bits such as High Needle or Needle , making it one of the best Bits available alongside Ball.
