## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/zap-bit/ | kind: description -->

First released with the UX-14 Scorpio Spear 0-70 Zap Starter as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Unique Line system on April 26th, 2025, Zap (or Z ) is a Balance type Bit. Zap features 16 Gears, similarly to Accel , and a small ball-shaped tip, similar to the one seen on Point . Therefore, the overall design of Z can be reminiscent of Level , but its performances and behavior are very different.

## Valutazione esterna (opinione)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/zap-bit/ | kind: opinion -->

Having poor stamina as well as high self knock-out risk, Zap is a very risky Balance type Bit. The high self knock-out risk may even become frustrating, especially for beginners. As such, this option may not be used by competitive players. Therefore, while not a must-have, Zap is a nice addition in any Beyblade X collection, as it is the most agressive Balance type Bit at the time of writing.
