## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/kick-bit/ | kind: description -->

First released with Hells Reaper T4-70K in the CX-05 Random Booster Vol.6 as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Custom Line on April 26th, 2025, Kick (or K ) is a Balance type Bit. Kick has a an hexagonal tip with a gap in its center, reducing friction and thus saving more stamina for the end of the Battle. The hexagonal shape is meant to make this Bit versatile, allowing both attack and counter attack moves according to Takara Tomy. In reality, Kick’s behavior can be similar to Taper and Unite , while probably being less skill intensive than these two. This Bit is indeed easy to play, the hexagonal shape providing enough friction to create attack patterns, while remaining fairly docile and easy to control. This Bit will engage Battles aggressively, but it will quickly switch to circular movements, typical of anti attacks combinations, a role in which Kick excels at. Furthermore, the hexagonal shape of the tip also provides stability, a trait that becomes crucial in Beyblade X gameplay mechanics. Therefore, Kick raw attack potential may not always be high enough to counter meta-game stamina combinations with high consistency, but it can be used to counter popular attack type combinations, such as Phoenix Wing 1-60R or Aero Pegasus 3-60R .

## Valutazione esterna (opinione)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/kick-bit/ | kind: opinion -->

Thanks to the hexagonal shape of its tip, Kick is probably the easiest balance type Bit available. Balance type Bits are often known for being quite skill intensive, requiring specific launching techniques to be as efficient as possible. This Bit seems to be just as effective as Taper or Unite, while being much easier to play, making it accessible to veterans and newcomers alike. Therefore, Kick is a strong addition in any Beyblade X collection.
