## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/assault-assist-blade/ | kind: description -->

First released with the CX-07 Starter Pegasus Blast ATr as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Custom Line on July 19th, 2025, Assault is an Assist Blade. Assault is entirely made of plastic, featuring three secondary contact points to support the Main Blade. Its shape has great synergy with the Main Blade Blast , making its primary contact points thicker. However, this Assist Blade, like all others options available, leaves three gaps underneath Blast. Additionally, this Assist Blade is slightly heavier than other options such as Charge , Slash or Jaggy , making it a decent option in Attack type combinations. The main purpose of Assault is to enhance Blast Smash Attacks , which can result in the sudden destabilization of the opposing Beyblade when successful. At the time of writing, this Assist Blade is an interesting alternative to existing options. However, in our opinion, it seems to be outclassed by the heavy, thick and smooth Assist Blade Wheel for competitive use.
