## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/slash-assist-blade/ | kind: description -->

First released in the CX-01 Dran Brave S6-60V Starter as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Custom Line system on March 29th, 2025, Slash is an Assist Blade Blade featuring a three-sided design. Slash is entirely made of plastic, and it has three main contact points, creating barrage attacks when combined with Main Blades such as Brave . Barrage attacks are a specific type of attack in Beyblade, meaning that the Bey will be able to perform consecutive hits, often resulting in higher Burst potential. However, the combination of plastic and metal contact points can make recoil feel more unpredictable. Slash three-sided agressive shape is clearly attack oriented, making it a better option for attack type combinations.
