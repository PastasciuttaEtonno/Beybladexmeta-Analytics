## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/leon-claw-blade/ | kind: description -->

First released in the BX-15 Leon Claw 5-60P Starter as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports system on October 7th, 2023, Leon Claw is a right-spin Blade featuring a pentagonal shape. This Blade has five contact points, each featuring an embossed claw design. The center part of the Blade features a lion head. While the shape and overall design of this Blade are intended to create decent attack and defense potential, its light weight prevents it from displaying consistent performances. Leon Claw is slightly heavier than the Dranzer Spiral Blade, the lightest Blade available in Beyblade X . This lack of weight results in poor stamina potential and fairly weak attack power. Furthermore, when it comes to defense potential, this Blade does not have Wizard Arrow Blade ability to deflect attacks from opposing Beyblades, getting easily destabilized instead, and the five-sided shape of the Blade does not create meaningful counter-attacks either. However, the light weight of the Blade creates great mobility, allowing the combination to easily ride the X-Line and perform the Xtreme Dash at high speed.
