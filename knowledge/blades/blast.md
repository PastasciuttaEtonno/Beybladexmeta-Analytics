## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/blast-main-blade/ | kind: description -->

First released with the CX-07 Starter Pegasus Blast ATr as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Custom Line on July 19th, 2025, Blast is a right-spin Main Blade featuring a three-sided design. This Main Blade shares similiraties with Aero Pegasus , featuring the iconic three-sided shape of the Pegasus line. Blast also has three gaps in order to facilitate air flow, albeit much smaller than the ones found on Aero Pegasus. However, unlike the latter, Blast main contact points are slanting downward, resulting in Smash Attacks (according to the Japanese definition) instead of upper attacks . Since the Custom Line Main Blades have to accommodate for the Assist Blades, the contact points are thinner when compared to Attack type Blades from the Basic and the Unique Line such as Aero Pegasus, resulting in less raw attack power. When compared to the latter, Blast has three thinner sections ont its contact points, creating three small gaps between itself and the available Assist Blades. Additionally, Custom Line Main Blades are significantly taller than regular Blades, exposing the Ratchet a bit more, thus increasing Burst risk. The taller height of the CX Blades also means they are easier to destabilize, a key gameplay aspect of Beyblade X.
