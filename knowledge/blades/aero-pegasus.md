## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/aero-pegasus-blade/ | kind: description -->

First released with the UX-00 Aero Pegasus 3-70A Rare Bey Get Battle Prize Booster as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Unique Line system on July 30th, 2024, Aero Pegasus is a right-spin Attack type Blade. This Blade is a reference to the Metal Fight Beyblade Series, more specifically the Pegasus Line. The overall shape of Aero Pegasus is highly reminiscent of the Pegasus Beyblades from the Metal Fight Generation, and the Gear Chip features the drawing of a Face Bolt in the background, right behind the Pegasus avatar. Unique Line Blades feature resin launcher hooks instead of the regular metal ones seen on Basic Line Models. This is meant to increase Outward Weight Distribution, a trait that significantly improves stamina and attack potential. Furthemore, the name “Aero” comes from the fact that the Blade has six gaps, three in the inner part of the Blade, three on the outer part. This results in improved aerodynamism, which increases stamina as well as Outward Weight Distribution. Unique Line Beyblades also have a gimmick, usually based on all components synergy. With Aero Pegasus, the gimmick is a “Double Metal Coat”. This simply means this Blade has two coats of paint instead of one : a blue coat and a green coat, resulting in the Blade unique color theme.

While Aero Pegasus is very rare Blade, thus making it very expensive to purchase, it is also one of the very best Blades the Beyblade X Generation has to offer.
