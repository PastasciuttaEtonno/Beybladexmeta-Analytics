# TyrannoBeat

## Profilo


**TyrannoBeat** è un Blade del sistema BX (Basic Line), di tipo attacco.
Il pezzo pesa 37,0 grammi e ha rotazione destrorsa.
Le statistiche dichiarate dal produttore sono attacco 65, difesa 30, resistenza 5. Sono valori del produttore, non misure di torneo: descrivono l'intenzione di progetto, non il rendimento reale.
Lo stesso pezzo circola con più nomi: **T.Rex** è lo stesso Blade, non un pezzo diverso, e nelle edizioni occidentali compare anche come Beat Tyranno. Una combo registrata come T.Rex e una registrata come TyrannoBeat sono la stessa combo.

## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/tyranno-beat-blade/ | kind: description -->

First released with BX-31 Random Booster Vol. 3 Tyranno Beat 4-70Q as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Basic Line system on April 27th, 2024, Tyranno Beat is a right-spin Attack type Blade. Tyranno Beat has an overall elliptic shape created by two large Blades, featuring a thick perimeter. Both Blades feature small protrusions to create an agressive design. However, the protrusions on both blades will wear down and flatten out over the course of battles. As a result, this Blade will gradually lose recoil and attack potential, eventually becoming a hybrid between Attack and Balance types. Furthermore, Furthermore, this Blade is relatively heavy, weighing around 37 grams, but this weight isn’t enough to provide a very high level of stamina due to Tyranno Beat’s elliptical shape. Additionally, unlike other elliptical Blades, Tyranno Beat remains easy to control, partly due to its high inertia. As a result, this Blade tends to struggle against top-tier Stamina-type combinations due to its lack of raw attack power. However, it can be used with some degree of success against various meta Attack combinations. Balance-type Bits can then be used to create decent anti-Attack combos.

## Valutazione esterna (opinione)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/tyranno-beat-blade/ | kind: opinion -->

This Blade has poor to average stamina and lacks attack potential, making it outclassed for competitive use. However, it is a great alternative to experiment with in order to create combos specialized in the art of counter-attacking. As a result, this Blade is a nice addition to any Beyblade X collection.
