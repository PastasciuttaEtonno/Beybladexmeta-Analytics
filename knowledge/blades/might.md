## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/might-main-blade/ | kind: description -->

First released in the CX-11 Emperor Might Deck Set as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Custom Line system on November 1st, 2025, Might is a right-spin Balance type Blade. At the time of writing, Might is one of the heaviest Main Blades from the Custom Line, alongside Blast . It is meant to be an upgrade for Wizard Arc , replacing Arc . This Main Blade has an overall circular shape, featuring 8 primary contact points. While officially labelled as a Balance type Main Blade, in reality Might leans more toward a Defense/Stamina type. When combined with the Assist Blade Wheel , Might becomes one of the best options from the Custom Line for Defense oriented combinations. This Blade also has decent stamina. It is actually described by Takara Tomy as a Blade featuring 8 sharp blades, meant to slowly drain the opposing Beyblade stamina with consecutive hits. However, Blast “Smash Attack” style, created by its three downward slanting contact points, seems to be more efficient to Destabilize the opposing Beyblade. Even when combined with Wheel, we had better results with Blast against top tier, competitive stamina type combinations. However, Might was still able to win some Battles in our testing sessions, albeit with less consistency than Blast.

## Valutazione esterna (opinione)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/might-main-blade/ | kind: opinion -->

While more testing and feedback are required, Might is definitely one of the best options from the Custom Line for Stamina/Defense type combinations. However, its lack of Destabilization potential may make it slightly outclassed by Blast for competitive use. Therefore, it is a strong addition in any Beyblade X Custom Line collection.
