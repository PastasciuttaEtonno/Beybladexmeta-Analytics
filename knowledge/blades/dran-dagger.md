## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/dran-dagger-blade/ | kind: description -->

First released in the BX-20 Dran Dagger Deck Set as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports system on November 2nd, 2023, Dran Dagger is a right-spin attack Blade. Dran Dagger has a circular yet aggressive shape, featuring six blades along its perimeter. The center part of the Blade features a dragon head exhaling fire. The six blades are intended to create multiple attacks in quick succession. In reality, when compared to its predecessor Dran Sword , Dran Dagger has a fairly similar weight, being slightly heavier than its predecessor (although weight can vary depending on the copies). It features a bit more stamina potential but it also has less raw attack power and smash attack potential. When used in traditional attack type combinations, using Bits such as Taper ( T ) or Flat ( F ), this Blade does not hit as hard as Dran Sword or Shark Edge , making the first crucial strikes of the battle less powerful. Therefore, this Blade tends to struggle a bit more than its predecessor against top tier Blades from the Xtreme Gear Sports system, such as Hells Scythe . Against top tier combinations using such Blades, Dran Dagger will most likely lose the battle but it is far from irrelevant, being able to score a good amount of points with consistency. A Blade such as Knight Shield can also mitigate the repeated attacks from Dran Dagger with relative ease. Nonetheless, this Blade has enough attack potential to knock-out and burst most of the Blades that are not considered as highly competitive. Overall, this Blade is a strong option when used in mobile attack type combinations, but it lacks the pure smash attack power of Dran Sword and Shark Edge.

## Valutazione esterna (opinione)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/dran-dagger-blade/ | kind: opinion -->

Dran Dagger can struggle against top tier Blades from the Xtreme Gear Sports system, without being outclassed either. It features a unique and interesting design, which could make this Blade a fairly versatile option. Furthermore, thanks to this design, there is a possibility that this Blade won’t suffer from wear as much as its predecessor Dran Sword . Therefore, Dran Dagger is a great addition in any Beyblade collection.
