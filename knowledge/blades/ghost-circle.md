## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/ghost-circle-blade/ | kind: description -->

First released with Ghost Circle 0-80GB from the UX-12 Random Booster Vol. 5 as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Unique Line system on December 28th, 2024, Ghost Circle is a right-spin Stamina type Blade. Unique Line Blades feature resin launcher hooks instead of the regular metal ones seen on Basic Line Models. This is meant to increase Outward Weight Distribution, a trait that significantly improves stamina and attack potential. Ghost Circle has a perfectly round, smooth circular shape meant to increase spin duration. However, Ghost Circle suffers from two critical weaknesses. It is one of the lightest Blades from the Beyblade X generation, weighing approximately 26,8 grams. Furthermore, the Blade is very small in size, and also slightly raised, exposing the Ratchet to opposing strikes. Therefore, a Ghost Circle combination can be easily knocked-out by Attack Type combinations. It can also be easily outclassed by heavier Stamina and Defense type Blades. However, the light weight makes it easier for this Blade to maintain its posture. Therefore, players may use Ghost Circle with some degree of success in spin-equalization combinations. Ratchets such as 9-60 and 5-60 , combined with the Bit Elevate , can be used to counter left-spin combinations such as Cobalt Dragoon 5-60 Elevate . Despite this interesting attribute, Ghost Circle is heavily outclassed by other options such as Wizard Rod and Silver Wolf for competitive use.
