## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/golem-rock-blade/ | kind: description -->

First released in the UX-13 Booster Golem Rock 1-60UN as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Unique Line system on January 25th, 2025, Golem Rock is a right-spin Defense type Blade. Unique Line Blades feature resin launcher hooks instead of the regular metal ones seen on Basic Line Models. This is meant to increase Outward Weight Distribution, a trait that significantly improves stamina and attack potential. Golem Rock has an overall hexagonal shape, featuring six primary contact points. These contact points have different shapes, alternating between three thick, semi agressive blades and three sharp, thinner blades. This design is meant to create counter attacks from a low stance/height with the Bit Under Needle . Weighing approximately 34 grams, Golem Rock could be too light to be relevant against heavy Attack type Blades. However, the increased Outward Weight Distribution, as well as the small size of the Blade are both resulting in a surprisingly decent Defense type option. Despite a lack of weight, this Blade can be used against good Attack type combinations with decent results. It has enough recoil to repel opposing Beyblades, and enough stamina to out-spin many attack type options. However, while Golem Rock can find niche use in “anti-attack” combinations, it cannot compete with top tier Attack type Blades, such as Shark Scale and Aero Pegasus . Furthermore, its stamina potential makes it heavily outclassed by Stamina type otions such as Wizard Rod and Silver Wolf for example. Therefore, this Blade cannot be recommended for competitive players, although it can be a strong, enjoyable and versatile option in a non-competitive environment. Golem Rock can be used in any type of combinations with some degree of success : Defense, Attack and Stamina. Using this Blade in Balance type combinations can be rewarding, since Bits such as Kick will make great anti-attack combinations. A Golem Rock/Kick combination will have the mobility, stability and knock-out resistance required to chase and counter Attack type combinations.

## Valutazione esterna (opinione)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/golem-rock-blade/ | kind: opinion -->

Golem Rock is a surprisingly good Blade, although it cannot be considered as top-tier. At the time of writing, it is one of the best Defense type Blades available. Therefore, while this Blade is outclassed for competitive use, it can be recommended to any Defense type player.
