## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/cobalt-drake-blade/ | kind: description -->

First released in the Rare Bey Get Battle Prize BX-00 Cobalt Drake 4-60F Booster as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports system, Cobalt Drake is a right-spin attack type Blade. Cobalt Drake is a heavy Blade, featuring a four-sided design with four main contact points. The center part of the Blade, the Gear Chip, features a drake. A drake is similar to a dragon without wings. The heavy weight of Cobalt Drake can be explained by the use of paint on the whole Blade, which can increase weight by 1 or 2 grams. If used in battle, the Blade’s paint will come off very quickly, and leave blue residue on stadiums and opposing Beyblades. Typically, the paint on the four main contacts points will come off, which results in minimal weight loss.

This Blade has four upward slanting contact points, but their design is not as aggressive as those found on Blades such as Dran Sword or Shark Edge . Intended to be an attack oriented Blade, Cobalt Drake semi-aggressive design and heavy weight make this Blade more balance oriented. This semi-aggressive design prevents the Blade from being overpowered due to its heavy weight, but it also makes it a highly versatile option. The inertia of Cobalt Drake can be used with some degree of success in any type of combination. Overall, this Blade is a jack of all trades, master of none capable of great performances.
