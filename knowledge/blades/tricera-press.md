# TriceraPress

## Profilo


**TriceraPress** è un Blade del sistema BX (Basic Line), di tipo difesa.
Il pezzo pesa 36,5 grammi e ha rotazione destrorsa.
Le statistiche dichiarate dal produttore sono attacco 20, difesa 65, resistenza 15. Sono valori del produttore, non misure di torneo: descrivono l'intenzione di progetto, non il rendimento reale.
Nelle edizioni occidentali il pezzo è distribuito con il nome Brace Triceratops.

## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/tricera-press-blade/ | kind: description -->

First released with BX-44 Booster Tricera Press M-85BS as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Basic Line system on June 28th, 2025, Tricera Press is a right-spin Defense type Blade. Tricera Press has an overall pentagonal shape, featuring five semi agressive primary contact points, creating a bumpy perimeter. Moreover, this Blade has a gimmick : it features thicker, larger launcher hooks, making them heavier as a result. This increases Inward Weight Distribution, which in theory significantly improves defense potential, by increasing Knock-Out resistance. Unfortunately, the thick, semi-agressive shape of the five contact points can repel agressive Blades, although Tricera Press recoil can feel very unpredictable despite its increased central weight distribution. It doesn’t have enough recoil to Knock-Out low recoil Blades such as Wizard Rod and Silver Wolf , and it may have mixed results against Attack type combinations. While Tricera Press can repel opposing Beyblades, it can also be knocked-out early in Battle. Furthermore, it tends to struggle against top tier Attack type combinations, using Blades such as Shark Scale and Aero Pegasus . However, at the time of writing, Tricera Press is one of the heaviest Defense type Blades available, making it one of the best options in this specific type, although Knight Mail may feel like a more reliable option.

## Valutazione esterna (opinione)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/tricera-press-blade/ | kind: opinion -->

While Tricera Press increased central weight distribution can be very interesting in theory, its unpredictable recoil and overall lack of stamina make it heavily outclassed for competitive use. However, at the time of writing, it is still one the best Defense type Blades available, making it a nice addition in any Beyblade X Collection.
