## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/reaper-main-blade/ | kind: description -->

First released with Hells Reaper T4-70K in the CX-05 Random Booster Vol.6 as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Custom Line on April 26th, 2025, Reaper is a right-spin Balance type Blade. Reaper has a four sided shape, featuring downward slanting contact points. This design is meant to create Smash Attacks according to the Japanese definition, or “Force Smash” Attacks in the US. This specific type of attack pushes the opposing Beyblade down on the stadium floor, resulting in a quick or progressive destabilization, depending on the design and size of the contact points. However, Reaper contact points are thin, resulting in a lack of raw attack power. All four main contact points have an additional smaller Blade, which creates a continuous attack pattern, close to Barrage attacks. Therefore, a Reaper combination tends to slowly destabilize the opposing Beyblade throughout the course of the Battle. Additionally, Custom Line Main Blades are significantly taller than regular Blades, exposing the Ratchet a bit more, thus increasing Burst risk. The taller height of the CX Blades also means they are easier to destabilize, a key gameplay aspect of Beyblade X. While this Main Blade has a three-sided shape, it has a grand total of nine contact points . More specifically, it features six metal contact points and three upward slanting plastic contact points. Reaper has an average stamina potential, enough to be used against Attack and Defense oriented combinations. This Main Blade can be used to destabilize Defense type Beyblades, and provide the right amount of attack and stamina to counter Attack type combinations.
