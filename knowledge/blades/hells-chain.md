# HellsChain

## Profilo


**HellsChain** è un Blade del sistema BX (Basic Line), di tipo equilibrio.
Il pezzo pesa 33,2 grammi e ha rotazione destrorsa.
Le statistiche dichiarate dal produttore sono attacco 35, difesa 40, resistenza 25. Sono valori del produttore, non misure di torneo: descrivono l'intenzione di progetto, non il rendimento reale.
Nelle edizioni occidentali il pezzo è distribuito con il nome Chain Incendio.

## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/hells-chain-blade/ | kind: description -->

First released in the BX-21 Hells Chain Deck Set as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports system on November 2nd, 2023, Hells Chain is a right-spin balance type Blade. This Blade features a five-sided shape, creating five main contact points. Each contact point has two sections, the shorter sections each featuring a small gap. The center part of the Blade features a red horned skull. The five-sided shape of this Blade results in a slightly uneven weight distribution and a lack of balance.

## Valutazione esterna (opinione)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/hells-chain-blade/ | kind: opinion -->

Hells Chain is heavier than its predecessor, and it has enough qualities to challenge many top-tier combinations. Instead of being an upgrade to its predecessor Hells Scythe , it comes as a more defense oriented balance type Blade. Unfortunately, the thin perimeter of this Blade can be exploited by opposing Beyblade to strike its underside and/or the Ratchet used. Therefore, it is important to keep the combination as low as possible to mitigate this weakness. Therefore, Hells Chain is a must-have in any Beyblade X collection.
