# SilverWolf

## Profilo


**SilverWolf** è un Blade del sistema UX (Unique Line), di tipo resistenza.
Il pezzo pesa 36,8 grammi e gira in destrorsa.
Le statistiche dichiarate dal produttore sono attacco 15, difesa 30, resistenza 65. Sono valori del produttore, non misure di torneo: descrivono l'intenzione di progetto, non il rendimento reale.
Nelle edizioni occidentali il pezzo è distribuito con il nome Sterling Wolf.

## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/silver-wolf-blade/ | kind: description -->

First released with the Silver Wolf 3-80FB Starter as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Unique Line system on October 12th, 2024, Silver Wolf is a right-spin Stamina type Blade. Unique Line Blades feature resin launcher hooks instead of the regular metal ones seen on Basic Line Models. This is meant to increase Outward Weight Distribution, a trait that significantly improves stamina and attack potential. Unique Line Beyblades also have a gimmick, usually based on all components synergy. This Blade has a free-spinning auxiliary plastic blade sitting right underneath the Blade itself. Silver Wolf has an overall circular shape, with three main contact points. At the time of writing, this Blade is considered as one of the best Blades of the Beyblade X Generation. Its weight and circular shape create high stamina potential, while the added free-spinning sub-layer tends to mitigate opposing Beyblade attacks, granting Silver Wolf a substantial defense potential as well. Furthermore, the free-spinning part combined with the smooth perimeter of the Blade create good L.A.D. (Life-After-Death). At the time of writing, this Blade is heavily used in official B4 (Takara Tomy) tournaments, usually combined with the Ratchet 3-60 (other options are viable). Its high stamina allows players to have a chance in stamina match-ups against Wizard Rod . Its decent L.A.D. can be used effectively against the spin-equalization combination Cobalt Dragoon 5-60 Elevate, and its good defense can counter some of the best Attack type Blades, such as Phoenix Wing and Aero Pegasus . Stamina type Bits are usually the favorite choice of competitive players, such as Ball , Orb , and Free Ball , although Elevate is the preferred Bit to counter Cobalt Dragoon 5-60 Elevate spin-equalization.
