# ClockMirage

## Profilo


**ClockMirage** è un Blade del sistema UX (Unique Line), di tipo resistenza.
Il pezzo pesa 37,7 grammi e ha rotazione destrorsa.
Le statistiche dichiarate dal produttore sono attacco 10, difesa 10, resistenza 80. Sono valori del produttore, non misure di torneo: descrivono l'intenzione di progetto, non il rendimento reale.
Nelle edizioni occidentali il pezzo è distribuito con il nome Mirage Clock.

## Descrizione (fonte esterna)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/clock-mirage-blade/ | kind: description -->

First released in the UX-16 Random Booster Clock Mirage Select as part of the Xtreme Gear Sports Unique Line system on October 11th, 2025, Clock Mirage is a right-spin Stamina type Blade. Unique Line Blades feature resin launcher hooks instead of the regular metal ones seen on Basic Line Models. This is meant to increase Outward Weight Distribution, a trait that significantly improves stamina and attack potential. Unique Line Beyblades also have a gimmick, usually based on all components synergy. This Blade is a reference to Basalt Horogium (Twisted Tempo) 145 Wide Defense, featuring similar edges along its perimeter. With a wide, circular shape, Clock Mirage is designed to have high stamina potential. In reality, Clock Mirage easily outclasses the best Stamina type Blades, such as Wizard Rod and Silver Wolf . However, in order to keep the game balanced, Takara Tomy introduced a new design on this Blade. First of all, it has a tall plastic frame, which can be reminiscent of Metal Fight Beyblade Spin Track, making this Blade the tallest at the time of writing. The tall height makes the Ratchet exposed and vulnerable, which severly increases Burst risk. Furthermore, it is only compatible with Ratchets featuring a “O” shaped lock, such as 9-65 or 4-55 for example. Therefore, Clock Mirage has limited choices when it comes to Ratchet options. At the time of writing, players will have to use 4-55 if they want to lower the combination’s height. Moreover, Clock Mirage is so tall that it can be used to avoid contact with the opposing Blade. A tall Bit such as Disk Ball for example will allow a Clock Mirage combination to avoid Cobalt Dragoon 5-60 Elevate and its Spin-Equalization potential. With attack type Blades such as Aero Pegasus and Shark Scale being popular in Beyblade X official tournaments, it remains to be seen if Clock Mirage outstanding stamina will be used. However, with such Stamina potential, Clock Mirage will have to be in competitive players mind in the future. For example, this Blade could be a deterrent against the usual, massive use of Stamina/Spin Equalization combinations such as Wizard Rod 9-60 Ball and Cobalt Dragoon 5-60 Elevate.

## Valutazione esterna (opinione)

<!-- provenance: third-party | source: https://beyblade.wiki/clock-mirage-blade/ | kind: opinion -->

While this Blade has been heavily criticized by a part of the Beyblade community, it is a brilliant release. Takara Tomy created a Blade with major strengths and weaknesses, showing a real effort to keep the game balanced and strategic, as it should be. As mentioned above, the exceptional Stamina potential of Clock Mirage makes it a fantastic addition to any Beyblade X collection. It is a very important release, that could affect Beyblade X meta game on the long term, due to its ability to easily couter some of the best, safest top tier Stamina type combinations.
